Asia

Asia is the world's largest continent, covering one-third of Earth's land across 48 countries. It is home to two-thirds of the human population, including the most populous nations, China and India. Famous geographic features include the Himalayas, the highest mountain range on Earth, and the high Tibetan Plateau. Asia is the biggest continent with 48 countries. Most of the world's people live here, including in China and India. It has the world's highest mountains, the Himalayas.


Africa

Africa is the second-largest continent and has 54 countries, more than any other continent. It features the world's longest river, the Nile, and the largest hot desert, the Sahara. This warm continent is famous for diverse wildlife, including lions, elephants, zebras, and rhinos. Africa is the second biggest continent and has 54 countries. It has the world's longest river, the Nile, and the giant Sahara desert. Warm Africa is famous for wild animals like lions, elephants, and zebras.


North America

North America is the third-largest continent, comprising 23 countries including Canada, the USA, and Mexico. It features famous natural sites such as Yellowstone National Park and the Grand Canyon. The human-made Panama Canal marks its southern border with South America. North America is the third biggest continent with 23 countries, like the USA, Canada, and Mexico. It has famous natural places like Yellowstone Park and the Grand Canyon. The Panama Canal separates it from South America.


South America

South America is the fourth-largest continent and is made up of 12 independent countries. It contains the Amazon rainforest, the dry Atacama Desert, and the towering Andes Mountains. Unique animals like llamas, capybaras, and anacondas live here. South America is the fourth biggest continent with 12 countries. It has the Amazon rainforest, the driest desert, and the long Andes mountains. Special animals like llamas and anacondas live here.


Antarctica

Antarctica is the fifth-largest continent, located at the South Pole and covered almost entirely in ice. Its extreme freezing climate bounce solar heat back into space, preventing permanent human settlement. Only visiting scientists, along with animals like seals and seabirds, brave the cold. Antarctica is the fifth biggest continent at the South Pole. It is covered in ice and extremely cold. No people live here permanently, but seals and visiting scientists do.


Europe

Europe is the second-smallest continent, containing roughly 51 independent countries. It holds both the world's largest country by land area, Russia, and the world's smallest country, Vatican City. Europe is widely celebrated for its rich history, distinct languages, and ancient castles. Europe is the second smallest continent with about 51 countries. It has the world's biggest country, Russia, and the smallest, Vatican City. Europe is famous for its many languages and old castles.


Australia

Australia is unique because it is the only continent made up of just a single country. Located between the Indian and Pacific Oceans, it is also referred to as Oceania or Australasia when nearby island nations are included. Well-known landmarks include the Sydney Opera House and the Great Barrier Reef. Australia is special because it is the only continent with just one country. It sits between two oceans. Famous spots include the Sydney Opera House and the Great Barrier Reef.
