New York City

Exploring the Five Boroughs, Diversity, and Landmarks

Vocabulary Words

  • Borough - one of the five main sections of New York City, which are also individual counties one of five parts of New York City that is also its own county
  • Diversity - a mix of many different cultures, languages, and backgrounds having many different kinds of people and cultures together
  • Landmark - a famous building or location that is easily recognized a famous place or building that lots of people know

The Five Boroughs

New York City is the largest city in the United States, and it is divided into five big sections called boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island. Here is a fun state secret: each of these five boroughs is actually one of New York State's 62 counties! New York City is split into five big parts called boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island. Each borough is also one of New York State's 62 counties!


Culture, Business, and People

New York City is famous all over the world for its cultural diversity. Millions of families have moved here from all over the globe, bringing their unique languages, music, holiday traditions, and delicious foods into special city neighborhoods. New York City has people from all over the world. Families bring their own languages, music, and food, making the city very diverse.


It is also a busy hub for all kinds of businesses! Millions of people work in NYC every day doing jobs in clothing fashion, television studios, restaurant cooking, big banks, computer technology, and art. New York City is full of many different businesses. People work in tv shows, bank offices, clothing design, art, and cooking.

Famous Landmarks to Visit

There are so many iconic landmarks to explore across the boroughs! You can visit the Statue of Liberty welcoming ships in the harbor, walk through the huge grassy fields of Central Park in Manhattan, or see thousands of animals at the world-famous Bronx Zoo. There are many famous landmarks in the city! You can see the Statue of Liberty, play in Central Park, or see animals at the Bronx Zoo.
