How New York State is Divided

New York is a huge state, so it is divided into 62 smaller areas called counties. Each county has its own local government to take care of communities by fixing roads, running public schools, and keeping neighborhoods safe. New York State is split into 62 smaller areas called counties. Each county has local leaders who take care of roads, schools, and police.


Counties are all different from one another, mostly because of population density. Some counties are urban with busy cities packed full of people, some are suburban with quiet neighborhoods, and others are rural with open farms and forests. Albany - The Capital City

Albany, located in Albany County along the Hudson River, is the capital of New York State. This is where state leaders meet inside the famous New York State Capitol building to write laws and make important decisions for everyone in the state. Albany is the capital city of New York State. State leaders meet in the State Capitol building in Albany to make laws for everyone.

Every county in New York has special attractions that make it unique!
